Cough Analysis

Detects whether a recording contains coughs; optionally estimates wet vs dry cough type for clinician review. Does not diagnose COVID-19 or any other disease.

Not a medical device. Investigational software; not cleared or approved by any regulator.
(opaque account ids, set by the login in deployment; recorded in the audit trail)

Input

Cough 2-3 times in a quiet place, about 20 cm from the microphone. Recording stops after 10 s.

webm, ogg, mp4, m4a, wav, mp3, aac, flac, 3gp or amr. The real format is checked on the server.

Recordings from the test split, never used for training. The true label is shown next to the result.
